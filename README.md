# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data of a coffee shop to gain **actionable insights** that can enhance performance and decision-making.  
The analysis focuses on sales trends, customer behavior, and product performance using **Power BI dashboards** and **data visualization techniques**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Understand **sales variation by day of week and hour of day**
- Identify **peak times for sales activity**
- Calculate **total monthly sales revenue**
- Compare **sales across different store locations**
- Find **average price/order per person**
- Highlight **best-selling products** (quantity & revenue)
- Analyze **sales by product category and type**

---

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights
- **Total Sales:** $6,98,812.33  
- **Total Footfall:** 149,116  
- **Average Bill/Person:** 4.69  
- **Average Order/Person:** 1.44  

### Key Visuals:
- Line chart: *Quantity ordered by hour* (peak at 9–10 AM)  
- Pie chart: *Category % distribution* (Coffee beans 39%, Tea 28%, Bakery 12%)  
- Bar chart: *Sales & Footfall by store location* (Hell’s Kitchen highest sales)  
- Top 5 Products: *Barista Espresso, Brewed Chai Tea, Hot Chocolate, etc.*  
- Orders by Day: *Sunday highest orders, Saturday lowest*  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI** – Dashboard creation & visualization  
- **Excel** – Data cleaning & preprocessing  
- **SQL/Python (optional)** – For advanced analysis  

---

## 📈 Insights
- Morning hours (9–10 AM) are peak sales times.  
- Coffee beans & tea contribute ~67% of total sales.  
- Hell’s Kitchen store generates the highest revenue.  
- Barista Espresso is the top-selling product.  
- Average customer spends ~$4.69 per visit.
